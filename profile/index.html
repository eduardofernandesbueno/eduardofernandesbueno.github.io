  6. 2024

    INMET work expanded into BigQuery, Python, machine learning, neural-network experiments and dashboards, alongside mobile-development activities at UEL.

  7. 2025

    Scientific and technical specialization

    Completion of Data Science studies and UEL master’s work brought together pattern recognition, applied research, AppEvasão, HCI and full-stack development.

  8. 2026

    Engineering, research and leadership converge

    UNIDATA, the data lakehouse MVP, ICAP-WEB-GP, SBIE research and software-project management connect institutional data engineering with technical guidance for undergraduate team members.

Documented overlapping UEL activities: front-end mobile development, May 2024–October 2025; full-stack web/mobile/backend development internship since October 2025; R&D and data engineering at ATI/UEL since March 2026. These periods are not added together as cumulative experience.

Tech Lead, Project Manager or Data Scientist?

These are complementary dimensions of technological work, not mutually exclusive identities. Technical leadership emphasizes shared understanding, engineering decisions, guidance and review. Project management connects scope, requirements, risks, stakeholders, quality and acceptance. Data science contributes experimental formulation, analytical methods and evaluation.

The connecting specialization is data: how information is collected, organized, governed, analyzed and made useful. Research provides methods and explicit uncertainty; architecture relates components and constraints; management and leadership make collaboration and delivery possible. The scope of each responsibility remains tied to the evidence described above.

My work integrates engineering and architecture, management and leadership, research and innovation, data science and AI, governance and institutional understanding. Programming and experimentation are instruments for conceiving, implementing, validating and evolving technological solutions.

This trajectory supports work at the interface between technical teams, researchers, managers and stakeholders, with scientific rigor, technological responsibility and attention to deliverables. The diagrams describe connections, not measured shares of identity or a certified level of competence.
